Congee

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Char Siu slices
  • Youtiao
  • Condiments

Steps

  1. Rinse and prep

    2. Rinse your rice thoroughly until the water runs clear. For an even creamier texture, you can soak the rice for 30 minutes or freeze the damp grains for a few hours to help them break down faster.

  2. Simmer the base

    3. Use a ratio of roughly 1 part rice to 7–9 parts liquid. Bring the rice and stock (with a few ginger slices) to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer.

  3. Stir for silkiness

    4. Cook for 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent the bottom from scorching. As the rice breaks down, the mixture will thicken into a smooth, pearlescent porridge.

  4. Prepare the toppings

    5. LWhile the congee simmers, soft-boil your eggs (6–7 minutes), slice your BBQ pork, and fry the shallots until golden and crisp.

  5. Assemble and season

    6. Ladle the hot congee into a bowl. Arrange the pork, egg, and ginger on top. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil, then serve with the crispy dough crullers on the side.
