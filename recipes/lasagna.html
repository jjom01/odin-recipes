Lasagna

    Ingredients

  • Meat sauce (Ragú)
  • Lasagna sheets
  • Cheese
  • Béchamel sauce

    Steps

  1. Make the meat sauce

    2. In a large pot, add brown ground beef (or a beef and sausage blend). Drain the excess fat. Add chopped onions, carrots, celery, and garlic, and cook until soft. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and seasonings like salt, pepper, oregano, and a pinch of sugar to balance the acidity. Simmer on low heat for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors deepen.

  2. Prepare the creamy filling

    3. Melt butter in a saucepan, stir in flour to make a paste (roux), and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in warm milk until a smooth white sauce forms. Simmer until thickened, seasoning with salt and nutmeg.

  3. Handle the pasta sheets

    4. If using traditional dry lasagna sheets, boil them in a large pot of salted water according to the package directions until al dente. Drain well and lay them flat on parchment paper or a clean kitchen towel so they don't stick.

    If using "no-boil" or oven-ready noodles, you can skip this cooking step.

  4. Assemble the layers

    5. Lightly grease a 9x13 inch baking dish. Start by spreading a small amount of meat sauce on the bottom to prevent sticking. Create your layers:

    1. Lasagna noodles (overlapping slightly)
    2. Your creamy filling (Béchamel or Ricotta mixture)
    3. Meat sauce
    4. Shredded mozzarella cheese

    Repeat these layers 2 to 3 more times. Ensure the top layer is finished with plenty of meat sauce and mozzarella.

  5. Bake and rest

    6. Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil (spray the foil with cooking spray so the cheese doesn't stick). Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and continue baking for another 15–20 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and the edges are golden brown. Crucial: Let the lasagna rest out of the oven for 15 minutes before slicing. This helps the layers set so you get that perfect structured square shown in the original photo.
