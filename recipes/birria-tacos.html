Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Consomé soup
  • Corn tortillas
  • Vegetables
  • Oaxaca cheese

Steps

  1. Prepare chilli paste

    2. Stem and de-seed the dried chiles. Boil them with the tomatoes, onion, and garlic until soft. Blend these with the spices and vinegar until perfectly smooth to create your adobo.

  2. Slow cook the beef

    3. Sear the beef chunks in a heavy pot or Dutch oven. Pour the chili paste and beef broth over the meat. Cover and simmer (or use a slow cooker) for 3–4 hours until the meat is fork-tender and falling apart.

  3. Shred and strain

    4. Remove the meat and shred it finely. Strain the liquid to create a clear, silky consomé.

    Tip: Save the reddish fat that rises to the top—this is what you’ll use to fry the tacos.

  4. Truss and roast

    5. Tie the legs together with kitchen twine (trussing) to ensure even cooking and a plump shape. Place the chicken on top of the vegetables. Roast in the oven for about 50–70 minutes (depending on weight). The chicken is done when the juices run clear and an instant-read thermometer hits 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh.

  5. Assemble and melt

    6. Add a layer of shredded cheese and the shredded beef to one half of the tortilla. Once the cheese is molten and the bottom is crispy, fold it over. Serve with a side bowl of hot consomé garnished with onion and cilantro.
