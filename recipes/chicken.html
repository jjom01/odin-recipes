Chicken

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken
  • Seasoning
  • Dried herbs
  • Vegetable bed

Steps

  1. Prep the bird

    2. Remove the whole chicken from the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking to bring it to room temperature. Pat the skin completely dry with paper towels; removing moisture is the secret to achieving that crisp, taut skin. Pre-heat your oven to 425°F (220°C).

  2. Seasoning and stuffing

    3. Generously season the cavity with salt and pepper, then stuff it with halved lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs like rosemary and thyme. Rub the outside of the chicken thoroughly with softened butter or olive oil, then apply a heavy coating of kosher salt, cracked black pepper, and dried herbs.

  3. Vegetable bed

    4. Toss your potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and red onion wedges in a bowl with olive oil, salt, pepper, and fresh thyme. Spread them in an even layer at the bottom of a roasting pan or large oven-safe skillet to act as a natural rack for the chicken.

  4. Truss and roast

    5. Tie the legs together with kitchen twine (trussing) to ensure even cooking and a plump shape. Place the chicken on top of the vegetables. Roast in the oven for about 50–70 minutes (depending on weight). The chicken is done when the juices run clear and an instant-read thermometer hits 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh.

  5. Rest

    6. Transfer the chicken to a carving board and let it rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing. This allows the internal juices to redistribute, ensuring the meat remains succulent and tender rather than spilling out onto the board. Garnish with fresh herb sprigs before serving.
